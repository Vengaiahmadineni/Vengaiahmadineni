## ✦ About

I build machine learning and statistical models that turn customer, transaction, and operational data into predictions. My work spans risk, fraud, retention, and segmentation across financial services and customer analytics, where I combine feature engineering, model validation, and stakeholder collaboration. I'm strongest at taking datasets through EDA, preprocessing, and hyperparameter tuning to models, with metrics and reusable pipelines that reduce recurring effort.
